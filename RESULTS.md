# Research Results and Evidence Boundaries

This document collects results recorded in the **submitted thesis** and the **selected aggregate artifacts** shared in this repository. It is **not** a new experimental run or a claim of independent reproduction. Primary clustering metrics, supplementary nested cross-validation estimates, and carbon held-out test scores evaluate **different targets** and must not be compared as model rankings.

## Supplementary models (distinct tasks)

| Analysis | Population and evaluation basis | Recorded result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Billing-based label transfer | 1,146 billing-matched households; adaptive nested out-of-fold evaluation | Random Forest balanced accuracy **0.5226**; macro-F1 **0.4727** |

| CO₂ regression | 1,656 carbon-qualified households; held-out test set | Histogram Gradient Boosting **R² = 0.3974**, **MAE = 46.26 kg CO₂/month**, **RMSE = 131.78 kg CO₂/month** |

### Interpretation of supplementary results

The billing model's target labels were generated by the original smart-meter clustering, so its score is **not** evidence that monthly billing directly measures temporal usage. The high-consumption classifier uses a sample-relative threshold; it is not an official tariff or welfare decision tool.

The CO₂ analysis estimates only **operational emissions associated with imported grid electricity** using a constant **0.71 kg CO₂/kWh** conversion, not an actual measured household total footprint or changing hour-by-hour grid emissions. The held-out regression model is better than its dummy-mean baseline on MAE (**46.26 versus 82.89 kg CO₂/month**), but has meaningful prediction error.

The held-out high-emission classifier records **59 true positives, 14 false negatives, 111 false positives and 148 true negatives** at its selected probability threshold **0.21**. Thus, relatively high recall comes with many false positives; its performance is not suitable for direct consequential household decisions.

These carbon model results can be inspected in [regression metrics](results/21_primary_continuous_emission_regression_heldout_test_metrics.csv) and [classification metrics](results/22_secondary_high_emission_classification_heldout_test_metrics.csv). See the corresponding model cards under [models/](models/) for thresholds, predictors, validation design, and cautions.

## Cross-source evidence and scope limits

The thesis uses monthly billing, household surveys and six-hour smart-meter registers to corroborate or contextualize the archetypes; these datasets have differing coverage and matching rates. They are neither comprehensive external validation nor household archetype ground truth. It is inappropriate to generalize these results to all Sri Lankan households or infer causes of behavior from the clusters or model explainability plots.

**Reproducibility limitation:** the public code is a curated, output-cleared reference set. Original household-level inputs, serialized models and certain diagnostic steps are withheld. The reported metrics have been **checked against submitted thesis text and saved selected artifacts**, but not regenerated by executing the public notebooks.
